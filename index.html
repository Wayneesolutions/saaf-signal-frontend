§02 — HOW THE NUMBER IS MADE

A confidence score is just a count of history repeating itself.

STEP 01

Find similar setups

Take today's chart — RSI, MACD, trend versus the 20 and 50-day averages. Search this stock's own history for moments that looked the same.

STEP 02

Count what happened next

For every matching setup, look at the next 5 trading days. Did the stock move the way we're forecasting? Yes or no — counted, not guessed.

STEP 03

That count is the score

If 68 out of 100 similar setups played out that way, we say 68%. If it's 51 out of 100, we say 51% and flag it as close to a coin flip.

No proprietary secret sauce. No neural network hallucinating price targets. Just backtested counts, shown to you exactly as they come out of the query.
